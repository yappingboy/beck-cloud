Amigurumi → knitting

Paste a crochet round pattern (“R2: inc x6 (12)” style). Rounds become knit rows: each round’s stitch count sets the panel width, and the shaping model is derived automatically.

Knit conversion

Gauge conversion keeps physical size: counts are rescaled from your crochet gauge to your knit gauge. Amigurumi shaping is distributed around the tube; split in two it becomes every-N-rows decreases at each edge — close enough to knit the piece flat and seam it.
