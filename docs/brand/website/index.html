What it does

Infrastructure that doesn't sell you out

BeckCloud is a homelab platform running 20+ services on a K3s cluster. Here's why that matters.

Your data stays yours

Every service is self-hosted. No third-party cloud providers, no data brokers, no "free tier" that costs you privacy. Your information lives on hardware you can audit.

Enterprise tools, homelab scale

Keycloak for SSO. Prometheus + Grafana for monitoring. Wazuh for security. Velero for backups. The kind of stack most small businesses wish they had.

Uptime you can verify

K3s cluster with automated backups, redundant storage, and real monitoring. 99.9% uptime target isn't marketing — it's measured, logged, and posted.

Zero tracking. Period.

No analytics. No cookies beyond what's necessary. No JavaScript tracking pixels. If you can't see it in the page source, it's not collecting anything.

GitOps everything

Infrastructure as code via Flux. Every config change is version-controlled, reviewed, and auditable. Even the homelab has a CI/CD pipeline.

Real services for real people

Password management, media streaming, file sync, project tracking, wiki, development tools. Everything runs behind SSO so you log in once and you're in.

About

A homelab with opinions

BeckCloud is one person's attempt to build the infrastructure they'd actually want to use. It runs on K3s, orchestrated with Flux, backed by ZFS, and monitored so thoroughly that even the monitor has a dashboard.

It's not a startup. It's not a product. It's a homelab with twenty services, a backup strategy involving more moving parts than most small businesses, and the quiet confidence of something that just works.

Everything here is open-source, self-hosted, and auditable. If it matters to you, it matters enough to run it yourself.
