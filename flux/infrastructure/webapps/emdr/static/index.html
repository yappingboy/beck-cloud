EMDR — Bilateral Stimulation

Two spheres will drift left and right in front of you. Track them with your eyes only, keeping your head still. A soft tone plays in the ear on the side the balls reverse.

Research standard dose: 1 Hz (one full left→right→left cycle per second) in 24–30 s sets. Slower (≈0.4 Hz) calms an overwhelmed client; faster (1–2 Hz) helps when processing stalls. Adjust below to taste.

Stop anytime: Esc (desktop) or your headset’s menu/system button (VR). Not a medical device — for research/practice use with a qualified professional.
